Why ordinary keyword search falls short: it only looks for your exact words. A question like "What steel is required in beams?" misses Clause 26.5.1.1 of IS 456, which talks about "tension reinforcement", and shows unrelated steel clauses instead. Smart search understands what you mean and keeps answers in the right engineering field.

How it works

From long standards documents to a clear answer in four simple steps.

You ask a question

Type it the way you would ask a colleague, or enter a reference like "IS 456 clause 26.5.1".

The app reads the standards

The standards are cleaned up and split into individual clauses, each labelled with its standard, clause number and year.

It finds the best clauses

It matches the meaning of your question, not just the words, and applies Civil, Electrical and Mechanical rules to keep answers relevant.

You get a clear answer

See the standard, clause, full clause text, a short summary and a recommendation on what to do.

PDF noise cleaning

Strip headers, footers, page numbers, watermarks and scan artefacts; repair hyphenation and encoding so clause text is clean UTF-8.

Structural chunking

Split on clause boundaries into 300–500 token chunks so each chunk carries one complete requirement, never a cut-off line.

Metadata anchoring

Attach standard code, title, clause number and year to every chunk so each answer is traceable to its source.

MiniLM embeddings

Encode chunks and queries into dense vectors with a MiniLM sentence-embedding model. This demo emulates the embedding space in the browser with engineering concept vectors.

Vector DB search

Retrieve the nearest chunks by cosine similarity from the vector store.

Hybrid re-ranking

Apply deterministic domain rules (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical), exact code and clause matching and intent anchors on top of similarity scores.

Answer assembly

Return code, clause, full clause text, a one-line summary and a compliance recommendation, with an expandable retrieval trace.
